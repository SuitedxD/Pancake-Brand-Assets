# Official Pancake Brand Assets

These are the Pancake brand assets, here you will find all the files previously shown in the official documentation, usage guidelines, and its direct download.

# Brand usage guidelines

## Recommended use

- Use the original assets linked on this page.
- Choose the logo variant that is legible against its background.
- Preserve the logo's original proportions and orientation.
- Leave enough surrounding space for the logo to remain distinct from nearby text and interface elements.
- Use the product logo for product-specific pages and the team logo for team-level communications.
- Use the provided social banner for the corresponding identity rather than substituting a different product's banner.
- Keep color usage consistent with the relevant product sample and check contrast before publishing.

## Avoid

- Stretching, squashing, rotating, or otherwise distorting a logo.
- Recoloring a logo or applying effects that are not part of an official variant.
- Adding unofficial outlines, shadows, filters, or decorative treatments.
- Placing a logo on a background where it loses contrast or becomes hard to recognize.
- Presenting a modified asset as an original or official logo.
- Using the team identity in a way that suggests an unofficial project is an official Pancake product, or implying endorsement or affiliation that has not been authorized.
